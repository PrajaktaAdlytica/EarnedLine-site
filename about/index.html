EarnedLine/about

A clearer record of what was earned.

EarnedLine is a research-stage concept for an inspectable evidence-and-approval loop.

Explore our approachContact EarnedLine
Research-stage concept · Illustrative visuals
01 / In context

Make the review inspectable.

Our proposed mission is a narrow evidence ledger: one billing/CRM pair, 20 contract-months, permissioned exports and human review before any action.

02 / In context

Give finance authority over the proposal.

The vision is a contract-to-invoice decision process that people can inspect and reverse. The product is organized around a record and decision rather than an opaque agent action.

03 / In context

This is a research-stage company concept.

No demand, real customers, live integrations or attributable recovery has been established. The next evidence is a finance-reviewed sample and an agreed paid reconciliation pilot.

04 / In context

A line worth following.

The EarnedLine name connects what was earned to the line that should appear in billing. Its visual system follows that path through documents, numbers and human decisions.

05 / In context

Start small. Earn the next scope.

Custom-contract mismatch is the first hypothesis. Entitlement assurance, renewals and collections follow only if verified leakage exceeds integration burden.
