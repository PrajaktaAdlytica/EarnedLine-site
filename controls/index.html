Attach dated spans, document identifiers, usage coverage and assumptions to the proposed discrepancy. Confidence labels must describe evidence quality, not invented AI accuracy.

03 / In context

Approval belongs to the right owner.

Finance validates the discrepancy. Contract/legal reviewers assess historical billability. Any correction, message or external write remains a separate authorized action.

04 / In context

An override leaves a trail.

A reviewer can dismiss, hold or reclassify a finding. The reason, owner and prior state stay visible; a proposed correction remains reversible.

05 / In context

A held action is an intentional state.

No automatic back-bills, dunning, customer messages, payment execution or billing/CRM/ERP/bank writes occur in this concept preview.

Put your question in context
Is EarnedLine an operating product?

EarnedLine is a research-stage product concept. This website and its interactive workbench use synthetic records. Production integrations, customer outcomes and recovery performance have not been demonstrated.

How is this different from failed-payment recovery?

A failed payment starts with an issued charge. EarnedLine explores a different question: whether an earned contract or usage obligation was omitted or incorrectly carried into billing. Retry tools and billing platforms remain important substitutes.

Read the workflow guide
What is included in the first pilot?

The proposed starting scope is one billing/CRM pair via permissioned exports and a clause-to-invoice ledger for 20 contract-months. Select contract reconciliation first; agree the exact sample and handling under NDA.

Can a candidate amount be back-billed?

A discrepancy is not a collectible amount. Finance must validate the finding, existing credits must be reviewed, and contract/legal approval is required before any historical back-bill is considered. Nothing is automatically issued.

Do you send customer messages or change billing systems?

No invoices, customer messages, payments or billing/CRM/ERP/bank writes execute through this website or the synthetic demo. Any future outbound message or system write requires a separately approved scope and human authority.

What systems do you integrate with?

No production integrations are claimed. A proposed pilot begins with agreed exports from one billing system and one CRM, plus signed terms and relevant usage or adjustment evidence. Mapping and integration burden are part of the evaluation.
