01 / In context

Write down the baseline first.

Capture current exception reports, analyst-known items, volume and review time. Without this baseline, a finding cannot be described as incremental.

02 / In context

Let finance adjudicate each candidate.

Review every item as already known, invalid, newly confirmed, held or future-preventable. Do not treat a model confidence score as ground truth.

03 / In context

Keep new and known findings separate.

Material previously unknown discrepancies are the central hypothesis. Known exceptions, duplicate findings and invalid candidates remain visible and excluded from incremental value.

04 / In context

Account for false positives and omissions.

Record the reviewed sample denominator, invalid candidates and finance-identified missed items. A polished demo provides no measured accuracy evidence.

05 / In context

Include the work needed to get there.

Integration, mapping and reviewer effort reduce the value of a finding. Evaluate gross margin and practical ownership rather than estimating revenue from list prices.

06 / In context

Two different kinds of opportunity.

Future prevention is a dated configuration or process correction. Historical billability needs separate contract/legal assessment and may be uncollectible.

07 / In context

Collection needs its own evidence.

Track confirmed, invoiced and actually collected amounts as separate stages. Credits, adjustments and timing must be reconciled before attributing any cash recovery.

08 / In context

A paid pilot is the decision gate.

Continue only with validated material new gaps, practical buyer ownership and a paid commitment with data access. Stop if substitutes cover the same work or ambiguity makes action unsafe.

Before the pilot

Questions worth settling first.

Product status, scope and decisions—without the fine-print surprises.

Put your question in context
Is EarnedLine an operating product?

EarnedLine is a research-stage product concept. This website and its interactive workbench use synthetic records. Production integrations, customer outcomes and recovery performance have not been demonstrated.

How is this different from failed-payment recovery?

A failed payment starts with an issued charge. EarnedLine explores a different question: whether an earned contract or usage obligation was omitted or incorrectly carried into billing. Retry tools and billing platforms remain important substitutes.

Read the workflow guide
What is included in the first pilot?

The proposed starting scope is one billing/CRM pair via permissioned exports and a clause-to-invoice ledger for 20 contract-months. Select contract reconciliation first; agree the exact sample and handling under NDA.

Can a candidate amount be back-billed?

A discrepancy is not a collectible amount. Finance must validate the finding, existing credits must be reviewed, and contract/legal approval is required before any historical back-bill is considered. Nothing is automatically issued.

Do you send customer messages or change billing systems?

No invoices, customer messages, payments or billing/CRM/ERP/bank writes execute through this website or the synthetic demo. Any future outbound message or system write requires a separately approved scope and human authority.

What systems do you integrate with?

No production integrations are claimed. A proposed pilot begins with agreed exports from one billing system and one CRM, plus signed terms and relevant usage or adjustment evidence. Mapping and integration burden are part of the evaluation.
