01 / In context

An existing charge can fail.

In failed-payment recovery, the invoice or charge exists and collection did not complete. Payment-recovery tools operate at that stage; retries do not establish whether all earned obligations were invoiced.

02 / In context

An obligation can be missing from billing.

A signed usage term may exist even when the invoice omits its corresponding line. Review clause, coverage, invoice and adjustments before calling that omission a discrepancy.

03 / In context

Locate the boundary in the example.

Acme Analytics has 12,000 metered events, 10,000 included and a $0.04 extra-event term. Invoice I-88 contains only its $2,000 base fee. The $80 gap is an unverified candidate, with credit applicability still open.

04 / In context

Compare with existing tools first.

Stripe Billing, Chargebee, accounting reconciliation and revenue analysts may already surface equivalent exceptions. Choose an additional review only when a previously unanswered question remains.

Public source notes

Vendor-authored sources establish capabilities and workflow overlap. They do not demonstrate demand, incremental recovery or superiority for EarnedLine.
