01 / In context

Select twenty contract-months deliberately.

Choose a bounded sample with negotiated terms and usage charges. Agree the billing/CRM pair and periods so the review can expose realistic mapping and interpretation effort.

02 / In context

Prepare four source groups.

Signed agreements and amendments, usage and entitlements, invoice lines, and credit/adjustment records support the evidence path. Keep original source IDs and dates.

03 / In context

Agree permission and handling.

NDA, rights to provide records, purpose, access, retention and deletion must be agreed before source data moves. There is no source-record upload through this website.

04 / In context

Name the reviewers.

Finance supplies discrepancy ground truth. Contract/legal owners resolve ambiguity and historical billability. A budget owner makes the purchase decision.

05 / In context

Preserve the baseline.

Bring existing reports and known exceptions, plus sample volume and time spent today. They distinguish incremental findings from work your stack already performs.
