01 / In context

Check which obligation the credit addresses.

A credit can relate to a different period, a service concession or an already-applied adjustment. Inspect its source and allocation before subtracting it from a new candidate.

02 / In context

Inspect prior application.

An unapplied credit and an adjustment already reflected in invoice or cash records have different effects. Preserve allocation evidence to avoid counting the same credit twice.

03 / In context

Make the net branch conditional.

The fictional gross candidate is $80. If CR-40 applies to that obligation and was not previously applied, the conditional net is $40. While applicability is pending, do not present $40 as collectible.

04 / In context

Keep billability separate.

Even a finance-confirmed net discrepancy does not prove an enforceable historical charge. Contract/legal review decides whether a correction is permitted; collection needs later evidence.
