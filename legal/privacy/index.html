01

Scope of this preview

This website introduces the EarnedLine research-stage concept and an isolated synthetic workbench. Public product examples use fictional entities and records. This notice describes the implementation currently shown; operational pilot handling requires a separate agreement.

02

Inquiry context and consent

The preview intake forms validate locally. No request-delivery endpoint is configured, so entered contact information is not sent or retained by the forms. Purpose-specific consent and a separate optional updates checkbox are shown for the future connected workflow.

03

Source records

Do not enter signed contract text, customer identifiers, invoices, event feeds or financial records through public forms. Any real sample needs authority to provide records, NDA and agreed access, purpose, retention and deletion before transfer.

04

Events and local behavior

This preview creates a bounded in-memory event journal for route views, selected offer IDs, demo and evidence interactions, pricing views and form actions. It contains no raw case records, amounts, email addresses or form text and is cleared on page reload. No analytics service receives it. The selected motion preference is stored locally in your browser. Source and campaign query parameters are limited to short strings.

05

Storage and service providers

This concept website is hosted as a private preview. Browser and hosting infrastructure may process standard request information needed to serve it. No certification, regional residency, retention policy or production service assurance is claimed. Final providers and retention terms must be documented before collecting real inquiry or pilot data.

06

Deletion and questions

The local form does not retain submissions. For a future connected workflow or permissioned pilot, agree a working contact and deletion process before collection. The contact page presents the inquiry route; delivery is not connected in this preview.

07

Draft status

This is a draft implementation notice dated 13 October 2026, subject to matching the final legal entity, collection behavior, service providers and counsel-reviewed terms before an operational launch.
