01

Concept-preview scope

EarnedLine is a proposed evidence-and-approval workflow. The website and synthetic demo are not an operating revenue-recovery service. All people, records, amounts and outcomes shown in the demo are fictional.

02

Permission and responsibilities

A real pilot requires an agreed scope, a permitted sample and authority to provide the relevant records. NDA and handling terms must be settled before transferring source documents. Public forms are not a channel for those documents.

03

Review authority

Finance reviewers validate discrepancies and exclusions. Contract/legal owners assess ambiguous terms and historical billability. A candidate amount, a confirmed discrepancy, an approved correction and collected cash are distinct states.

04

External actions

The preview issues no invoices, sends no customer messages, collects no payments and writes to no billing, CRM, ERP or bank system. Never send back-bills without contract/legal approval. Any future outbound message requires approval of its balance, recipient and wording.

05

Commercial hypotheses

The $3,000 fixed pilot and $2,000–$6,000 per month single-stack prices are test hypotheses. They are not current checkout offers, validated market prices or a binding commercial commitment. Any paid pilot requires a separate agreed price, scope and decision.

06

Outcomes and assurance

No recovery, ROI, compliance, certification, integration or eligibility outcome is guaranteed or established by the concept preview. Existing platforms and current analyst processes may provide equivalent exceptions. Pilot continuation depends on finance-validated incremental value and a paid commitment with licensed access.

07

Draft status

This responsibility statement is a draft dated 13 October 2026. The legal entity, jurisdiction and binding commercial terms must be supplied and reviewed by counsel before an operational service launch.
